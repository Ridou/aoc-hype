The Origin

From Bartender to Congress

In 2018, a 28-year-old Bronx-born bartender and waitress shocked the political establishment — defeating a 10-term incumbent in one of the biggest primary upsets in American history. No one gave her a shot. She won anyway.

1989
Born in the Bronx, New York
Grew up in a working-class Puerto Rican family. Commuted from the Bronx to school in Yorktown Heights.
2011
Boston University — Cum Laude
Graduated with degrees in Economics and International Relations while working multiple jobs to support her family after her father's death.
2016–2018
Bartender & Community Organizer
Worked service industry jobs while organizing for progressive causes. Her brother nominated her to run for Congress.
June 2018
Historic Primary Upset
Defeated 10-term incumbent Joe Crowley — the #4 Democrat in the House and presumed future Speaker — with a grassroots, small-donor campaign.
January 2019
Youngest Woman Ever Elected to Congress
Sworn in at age 29. On day one, she joined protesters in Nancy Pelosi's office demanding action on climate change.
2019–Present
Reshaping American Politics
Consistently one of the most influential members of Congress, with a massive grassroots following and a record that punches well above her seniority level.

The Record

Real Wins for Real People

She didn't just show up — she delivered. Here's what AOC actually got done.

🌍
$369 Billion Climate Investment
Core tenets of the Green New Deal became the backbone of the Inflation Reduction Act — the largest climate investment in US history. 40% emissions reduction target by 2030.
💉
Insulin Cap at $35/Month
Fought alongside advocates to cap insulin prices at $35/month for Medicare beneficiaries and limit out-of-pocket drug costs to $2,000/year. Life-saving for millions.
💧
Lead Water Infrastructure
Doubled federal funding to replace lead pipes in schools and childcare programs. No child should be poisoned by drinking water in America.
👵
Home Care Workers Act
Championed fair wages legislation guaranteeing minimum wage and overtime protections for 3+ million home care workers — overwhelmingly women of color.
🌱
American Climate Corps
Her advocacy helped inspire the creation of the American Climate Corps, training 20,000 service members for climate resilience and clean energy jobs.
🏘️
$100M Green Infrastructure
Secured over $100 million in funding for 60+ Green New Deal infrastructure projects across the country, from Hawaii to Vermont.
🌊
Fastest FEMA Disaster Declaration
After devastating floods hit her district, she secured one of the fastest FEMA disaster declarations in history. ~25,000 families received $165 million in aid.
👨‍👩‍👧
Child Tax Credit Expansion
Fought for expanded Child Tax Credit provisions in the American Rescue Plan, providing monthly payments to working families and cutting child poverty nearly in half.
💳
Credit Card Rate Cap
Introduced legislation to cap credit card interest rates at 10%, protecting working-class Americans from predatory lending practices.

⚡
